The Challenge

The simplest hard thing you'll ever do.

The 100K Step Club is a monthly challenge (date is announced the month before) where you can log the most steps you've ever done in a day with a growing global community.

Walking is a seriously underrated form of focus and self-care. What we need more of in life enjoying the present moment as it unfolds at a walking pace.

The Founder

I'm Graeme.

2019: my friends bet me at a bar in Osaka that I couldn't walk 100,000 steps before midnight. They were right — I failed. Twice. I couldn't stop thinking about it. Four years later, I finally did it on my honeymoon. Then I did it again.

Now I'm doing this every month. Turns out the best ideas, the clearest thinking, and the best sleep of your life are all hiding behind a really long walk.
