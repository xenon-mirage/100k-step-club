Account & Data

Leave cleanly.

Delete the account. Delete the data. No scavenger hunt.

The fastest route is inside the app. If you cannot sign in, email from the address connected to your account and we will verify the request.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open 100K Step Club and choose Profile.
  2. Scroll to Account and choose Delete account.
  3. Read the deletion summary and confirm. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion without app access

Email graeme@100kstepclub.com with the subject Delete my 100K Step Club account. Send it from the email address connected to the account. If that is impossible, include your @handle; we will ask for enough information to verify ownership without requesting a password or health screenshot.

What deletion removes

  • Your account identity and profile, including handle, display name, discoverability, and profile photo.
  • Your stored daily step totals, badges, and Journey progress derived from those totals.
  • Your friend requests, accepted relationships, blocks, reports associated with your account, notification preferences, and device push tokens.

Timing and backups

In-app deletion begins immediately. Verified email requests are normally completed within seven days. Limited copies may remain temporarily in encrypted operational backups until the provider's routine retention cycle expires. Backups are not used for ordinary account access and are restored only for disaster recovery.

Need help?

Email graeme@100kstepclub.com. Never send your password, Apple or Google credentials, or a screenshot containing private health information.
