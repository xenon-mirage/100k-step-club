App support

Need a hand?

Tell me what happened. We'll take it from there.

Email graeme@100kstepclub.com for help with the app, your account or a safety concern. You don't need to sign in to ask.

Include the useful bits

Your phone model, iOS version, app build number if you know it, what you tapped and what happened. For step or fire timing, include the local date, time and timezone. A screenshot can help, but cover private names, email addresses and Health details first. Never send a password, sign-in code, or Apple or Google credentials.

Steps haven't arrived

Open the app and refresh Today while connected to the internet. Check that 100K Step Club has permission to read Steps in Apple Health. If access is off, you can turn it on in Health's profile settings under Apps and Services, then return to the app. The app can't read steps that Health hasn't made available. Your Journey starts when you join the club.

Can't sign in?

Use the same Apple or Google account you originally joined with. If a sign-in window was cancelled, try again. Still stuck? Email your club handle and a description of the problem; we'll help verify the account without asking for your password.

Crew and fire

Crew comparisons require an accepted friend request. Use Crew filter beside Tier race to choose whom you see; it doesn't change your friendships. You can send fire once to each person per sending day. Sending resets at your local midnight; when you travel, a timezone change may wait until the current sending window ends. Received fire counts as today according to your own local date. Please report a fire that repeats after you have already seen it.

Profile photos and safety

New profile photos wait privately for review. You can check the status or cancel a pending photo on You. To report a member, open their Crew profile and use Report; use Block to end their access to your Crew relationship. You can also email a concern or appeal to graeme@100kstepclub.com. Include the handle and a short description. Please don't email harmful imagery.

Your data and account

Open You → Settings to export your data or remove your account. Account removal cannot be undone. If you cannot get into the app, follow the account deletion instructions. Read the Privacy Policy for what is removed and the limited safety evidence that may be retained.

The next official day

The next event is Saturday, September 26, 2026. Find the details in Event or on the website. Only official event days count towards the global leaderboard; ordinary daily steps don't automatically create an official result.
